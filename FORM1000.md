# Form 1000 receipt optimiser (dry-run)

Local planner for two receipts. It reads a draft sheet you fill in and prints a combination. It does not submit Form 1000, does not assign orders, and does not place or create an order.

## Objective

Assemble **two receipts**, each with a **product total of at least HKD 1,500**. Product amounts exclude shipping, tax, and fees.

Site research also describes a Form 1000 rule of at least 2 orders with a **combined** product total of at least HKD 1,500 (see [FINDINGS.md](./FINDINGS.md)). This tool’s default objective is the pair of receipts at HKD 1,500 each.

Among feasible pairs it keeps the tightest product spend: the lowest combined total at or above the two thresholds, then the lower of the two receipt totals, then fewer lines. A product row is used on at most one receipt. Set `allowSplit` (or `splitQty`) only when a partial quantity is a real option; otherwise the row stays intact. `allowSplit` accepts a quantity up to 8.

If no pair exists, the result says so and shows the shortfall, including a best-effort split that is not a filing.

## Draft sheet

Copy [fixtures/form1000-sheet.template.csv](./fixtures/form1000-sheet.template.csv). [fixtures/form1000-sheet.example.csv](./fixtures/form1000-sheet.example.csv) is synthetic.

| Column | Role |
|---|---|
| `orderDate`, `orderNumber`, `sku`, `productName` | Labels. At least one of order number, SKU, or product name. |
| `qty`, `unitProductAmountHkd`, `lineProductTotalHkd` | Product money only. The optimiser sums `lineProductTotalHkd` (or `qty * unit` when the line total is blank). |
| `alreadyPlaced` | `true` rows are ignored. |
| `allowSplit`, `splitQty` | Optional. Without them the row is one piece. |
| `personalUse` | Optional check flag. Not a buying suggestion. |
| `shippingHkd`, `taxHkd`, `feeHkd` | Ignored if present. Not added to the product total. |
| `costHkd`, `retailHkd` | Optional transcription of inventory 成本 and 零售. Recorded on the line and **not** added to the product total. |

## Personal-use checks

The result reports two checks against the assembled receipts only (not a live balance):

- Personal use at most 30% of those receipts’ combined product total.
- Personal use on each receipt at most HKD 450.

A failing check is a warning. The tool does not recommend personal purchases to fill a gap.

## Commands

```bash
npm run form1000:optimise -- fixtures/form1000-sheet.example.csv
node cli.js form1000-optimise fixtures/form1000-sheet.example.csv
```

MCP tool `unf_form1000_optimise` accepts `fixturePath`, `csvText`, or `rows`. Same plan, still local, still no submit. `confirm` is ignored.

## Quarter windows

`quarterDate` values use `DD/MM/YYYY-DD/MM/YYYY`. The recorded deadline is the **last day of the quarter, 11:59 pm US Eastern**.

[fixtures/form1000-quarters.example.json](./fixtures/form1000-quarters.example.json) is a synthetic example. It is not a live account, balance, or order list.

```bash
node cli.js form1000-quarters read
node cli.js form1000-quarters record 01/01/2026-31/03/2026 --out /tmp/unf-quarters.json
```

MCP tool `unf_form1000_quarters` reads the example when `filePath` is omitted, and writes only when `action` is `record` and `filePath` is set. The file stores quarter dates and the Eastern deadline. It does not store personal balances.

## Where to copy numbers from

Two different screens. This repo does not scrape either one, and fixtures stay synthetic.

**Form 1000 unplaced orders.** `forms.unf1000` (`https://hk.unfranchise.com/index.cfm?action=forms.unf1000`). That list is orders not yet placed on Form 1000. A signed-in Node fetch can still be a login wall; do not save login-wall HTML as a fixture.

**On-hand inventory for a new sales receipt** (not the Form 1000 order list). Confirmed in a signed-in browse on 13 Nov 2026 (Asia/Hong_Kong). No customer, SKU, quantity, or price from that browse is stored here.

- Menu: 事業 → 我的顧客 → 銷售收據輸入. That is the live label. Conversation may also say 創造銷售收據.
- Landing: 優惠顧客選擇 at `moe.unfMOEPCSelect` (`https://hk.unfranchise.com/index.cfm?action=moe.unfMOEPCSelect`). Pick one preferred customer, then 繼續.
- Inventory: 產品存貨選擇 at `moe.unfMOEPCSelectU`. Columns: 產品, 代號, 成本, 零售, 可使用, 數量. 可使用 is on-hand quantity. Record both 成本 and 零售, then put the product amount that should count toward the HKD 1,500 total in `lineProductTotalHkd`.
- A hidden 手動產品代號 catalog lists many SKUs and has no 可使用 column. It is not on-hand inventory.
- The next 繼續, to `moe.unfMOEFinalPreview`, was not clicked. Nothing in this tool creates a receipt or submits that form.
